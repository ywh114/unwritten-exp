# Monster & Wonder Corpus v3

Everyday corpus = the normal encountered monsters (non-ecology). Majestic = hand-seeded singular entities at ley sites, non-hostile by default. Lifting provides edge texture. Monsters are not flora/fauna-split. Ecology-duplicating creatures are excluded — phylogeny + lifting owns that niche.

## B. Elemental submodule (spec stub)

Cosmopolitan monster family, generated rather than listed.

- **Forms:** slime, elemental, sprite, will-o'-wisp. One gimmick per form. (Salamander form dropped — collides with the ecology's salamanders.)
- **Elements:** open-ended. No fixed list; the element vocabulary is drawn from local fields (temperature, moisture, substrate) and site theme, so the family reads geography like the ecology does.
- **Naming:** on-the-nose compound — ember slime, frost elemental, spark sprite.
- **Mechanics:** still records + counters. A gimmick is a typed counter effect (acid dissolves, ember burns). Nothing per-tick. Possible custom mechanics left open.

## F. Taste rules

1. One gimmick, legible from the name; no footnotes, no explained jokes.
2. Overexposure budget per myth-source; Greek/Roman canon is spent.
3. Monsters must not duplicate what phylogeny + lifting generates.
4. Majestic = non-hostile by default; grandeur is not aggression.
5. Honest depth: plain surface; rigor lives in machinery, never in narration's claims.
6. Structure-bound monsters bind to features, never wild spawns.
7. Imply, don't declare.
8. A concept must not require dynamics the engine cannot generate; located means committed location fields.
